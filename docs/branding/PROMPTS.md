# Generation prompts

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. The icon was generated first; the banner used that icon as its image reference. Original PNG outputs are preserved in this folder.

## Primary icon

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: primary icon for Economy, an original Minecraft economy mod.
Primary request: create one polished, memorable game-mod emblem, a chunky gold coin bearing a highly legible block-built capital E. A single front-facing coin with a subtle visible lower-right thickness, a stepped octagonal silhouette, strong dark edging and a simple inset rim. The E is the unmistakable focal point, large and geometric.
Style/medium: original crisp pixel-art game icon, deliberate square pixel clusters, restrained highlights and shadows, flat front face. Make it feel hand-crafted for a block-building game rather than a cryptocurrency or fintech app.
Composition: one centered emblem, square canvas, emblem fills roughly 80 percent with generous transparent margin. The mark must remain readable at 32 and 64 pixels.
Text (verbatim): "E" only.
Constraints: real transparent background; no other text, no mockup, no surrounding objects, no Minecraft logo, no copied brand or bank/network marks, no watermarks, no tiny details, no glow, no gradients outside the coin.
```

## Banner

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: wide README header and project branding banner for the Minecraft mod Economy.
Input image 1: established primary icon, identity reference and supporting insert. Preserve its gold stepped coin and block-built E design, proportions and palette.
Primary request: create a polished horizontal brand lockup. Place the coin at the left, and the exact word "ECONOMY" prominently to its right in original chunky, squared cream lettering, carefully spaced and instantly readable. Beneath the title, smaller restrained uppercase text exactly "BANKING. CASH. COMMERCE."
Composition: wide 3:1 banner, preferably 1536 by 512 pixels. Generous margins. Flat deep forest-green backdrop (#122D26) with only a very subtle stepped geometric border detail. Coin is the only illustration; no scene, no additional symbols. Gold (#F4BE35) and warm cream (#FFF0C8) accents.
Style: crisp pixel-inspired game branding, clean and controlled, strong typography, no Minecraft franchise lettering imitation. Keep the coin faithful to the supplied mark, do not reinvent the E.
Constraints: exact spelling ECONOMY, no extra copy, no badges, no watermarks, no mockup, no charts, no currency signs. Entire text stays comfortably inside the canvas.
```
