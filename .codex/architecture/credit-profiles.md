# Credit profiles, scoring and underwriting — A6-07

## Persistence and first issuance

Schema V24 adds `credit_profiles`, `credit_score_history`, `credit_inquiry_groups` and `credit_inquiry_applications`, with owner-history, application-period and application-journal-frontier indexes. There are no seed profiles or production score/report jobs. Missing profile means UNESTABLISHED, rather than a fabricated 650 row. Current schema validation/backup/restore includes V24; old identities, money, tokens, worlds and immutable facts remain unchanged.

`CreditProfiles.Profile` records world/owner, current saved score, first real credit issuance UTC/facility/issuer, starter currency/minor allowance, policy version, revision, last scored boundary, reporting frontier and input fingerprint. First fields survive closure, token replacement and bank changes. No delete/reset API exists. The unique world/owner key and serialized absence check prevent another issuer from granting another starter.

`CreditProfiles.firstIssuance(owner,InitialRequest,processingClock,ActualIssuance)` is a trusted future A6-08 composition seam, not a player endpoint. Canonical request binds world/owner/session/issuer/facility, denomination currency/revision and calendar origin. Preparation rechecks store world, owner, issuer enabled, live denomination/origin and absence. Processing UTC supplies the first-credit instant. Callback receives `Starter` with score 650 and exactly 2,500 world-major units (including the explicit foreign-issuer starter currency exception).

Only a callback Commit with result `CREDIT_CARD_ISSUED` and the matching facility UUID composes profile, INITIAL history, actual facility/custody/money effects and the original outcome in one transaction. Null, rejected, unsupported or nonissuance callbacks create no profile or entitlement. The callback must perform real A6-08 admission, issuer/network/Tier/liquidity/exposure checks, register the real facility/custody and consume the allowance exactly. A session UUID alone is not authorization. No production service installs this initializer now; debit issuance never calls it.

V1 facts: `economy:credit-profile` (13 profile fields, base64url pipe-separated), `economy:credit-score-history` (18 history fields, same encoding) and `economy:credit-inquiry` (11 pipe-separated fields, including original operation UUID). Canonical kinds are `economy:credit-profile/economy:first-credit-issuance`, `/economy:accepted-inquiry` and `/economy:score-cycle`. Exact names are source constants. Validators preserve canonical numeric/UUID values, policy version, bounds, baseline/component consistency, snapshot SHA256 and bounded reason codes. New handlers retain 64-record/128KiB transaction budgets. No monetary record version changes.

## Deterministic version1 policy

`ScorePolicy.calculate` computes from fixed 650 baseline, never adds a delta to the previous score. Approved payment window 364 days, +4 met/−15 missed clamped−90..100; defaults 120/90/60 at ages<364/<728/<2,548 days, capped 360; age+2 per full 28 elapsed days capped 90; grouped accepted inquiries−5 per seven-day bucket over 84 days capped 30. Final score clamps 300..900. Payments of still-reportable defaulted facilities are suppressed. Payment/sale cannot reset default dates; expired default reports stop contributing at the original seven-year instant.

Report revisions are selected deterministically as of the boundary. Later-cure updates retain the original due+2 missed snapshot; factual corrections can change interpretation at a later recomputation while all earlier history stays immutable. Duplicate/conflicting report identity/revision/default dates fail closed. Future-effective reports do not affect the boundary. Snapshots capture counts, period/first date, exact utilization area/duration/provenance, source digest, policy version and reasoned outcome.

Utilization integrates aggregate normalized portfolio owed (principal+posted/accrued interest) divided by aggregate active limits across authoritative event-time changes. It does not average individual card ratios or use a cutoff balance. Closed/defaulted facilities are excluded according to authoritative source status. Every segment uses one captured common valuation unit and source fingerprint; future facility/report adapters own correct membership and immutable event-time input construction. Exact rational arithmetic compares 0/10/30/50/75/100% thresholds; no facilities earns 0 points, active zero debt earns+30. Missing coverage/provenance is unavailable, never zero.

`dailyAreas` exposes ≤29 versioned day-sized exact areas, active duration and partial coverage; `combineDaily` validates intervals/version/precision and rebuilds the cycle. Interval length is at most 28 days, raw lists ≤4,096 and rational precision ≤8,192 bits. A first partial cycle cannot contain more active duration than elapsed established credit. `SummaryInput` supports a constant-size result from a future durable paged report fold: long counts, ≤13 distinct world-week bucket IDs, exact utilization and immutable source digest. Raw and summary paths share the same component/snapshot formula; large counts use exact integer arithmetic before clamping. Future A6-10 must page/deduplicate/correct source reports before supplying a complete summary; no all-history production scan is installed here.

## Inquiry composition and historical frontiers

`acceptedInquiry` composes a trusted actual accepted underwriting Commit, including a business denial, with its immutable application receipt and at most one owner/world/week group. Malformed/unauthorized/unsupported Reject writes nothing. Inquiry recording can precede a profile without establishing credit. First-profile opening is exempt only through firstIssuance; an arbitrary firstIssuance boolean in an inquiry request is refused.

Accepted time comes from the serialized processing clock, not a client timestamp. Each application records original operation and actual journal sequence. `inquiryBuckets(...,maxJournalSequence)` performs ≤13 indexed existence probes using the true 84-day cutoff, accepted time and captured journal frontier. It handles world-origin acceptance, later acceptance in a boundary week and same-instant applications after a historical snapshot. The unrestricted overload is for current views; historical reporting/scoring must supply the captured frontier. Application UUID and original-operation replay cannot create another inquiry; grouping spans issuers and card/loan products.

## Dormant scoring and future activation

`CreditScoring.PRODUCTION_STATUS` is `DORMANT_REPORTING_UNAVAILABLE`. No provider is added to FoundationRuntime or DeadlineDispatcher. Ordinary startup/readiness has no missing reporting dependency and credit reads cannot schedule work. Production report-based recalculation remains unavailable; only detached fixtures supply reports in this package.

Future composition requires an installed `FinancialCalendar.Stage.REPORTING` source with completed due+2 frontier≥boundary, nonregressing frontier, exact source revision/first-issued date, captured fingerprint and supported historical policy. SCORE follows REPORTING. It rechecks profile revision and the next sequential world-aligned 28-day boundary, strictly after first issuance. Same-period/new-ID attempts cannot overwrite history or apply a delta twice; backlog cannot skip an incomplete earlier period. Policy2/unknown history is explicitly unavailable until its implementation exists. A6-10-03 must install and accept the combined reporting/scoring DAG and owner-scoped child operation IDs. The future scheduler overload uses narrow system authority rather than a forged player.

History preserves INITIAL/SCORE kind, boundary, period identity, policy/revision, full components, input snapshot/fingerprint/reasons and reporting frontier. Queries use exclusive keyset cursors, 1–16 records/page, and an indexed latest history read. Corrections affect future period results; stored history is not silently recomputed.

## Underwriting inputs and read APIs

`CreditUnderwriting` implements exact approved score bands, combined revolving ceiling/headroom, starter exception, bank/subprime minimums/terms/APRs, Tier2 multipliers and exposure limits. Score650 normal combined ceiling 5,000 leaves 2,500 after the first 2,500 facility. It counts open revolving exposure as max(limit,owed), plus loan/closed/collection liabilities once; uncollectable unpaid debt still counts until explicit extinguishment. Lower ceilings block new facilities/increases/draws without changing contracts or debt. Draw headroom uses facility units; aggregate exposure uses world units. No limit-reduction or lending mutation is exposed.

Native request helpers validate whole-major amounts in the actual contract currency, value exposure/principal upward in world minor units and convert maximum offers downward. One fresh immutable A2 snapshot supplies bounded positive rates; missing, future or>48h-old rates fail closed. Principal/limit calculations use checked integers and exact decimal APRs, never doubles. Subprime terms are independent of bank Tier. These pure offers are inputs, not approval/funding/issuer authority; A6-08/09 must combine current profile, blocker/valuation sources, actual branch/Tier and finite lender liquidity.

`LedgerService.creditProfile/creditHistory` require fresh owner grants. `inspectCreditProfile/inspectCreditHistory` use separate operator inspection grants and existing durable InspectionAudit plus live audience checks. No raw storage(actor) owner endpoint or new scoring mutation is exposed. Protocol 5 adds only credit_profile/credit_history/credit_next; banking surfaces show saved score/components/history or UNESTABLISHED. Register/admin/world-setup access cannot forge these owner controls. No login/debit/read/failed application initializes a profile. Application, credit facility, loan and recurring reporting/scoring activation remain later-package work.
