## Activity attribution and bounded storage

Compose observation facts/counters through `FoundationStore.decorateCommit` after original-operation lookup and existing financial preparation. Chain the deposit-yield observer; do not replace it. Observe authoritative semantic facts once: a register checkout containing merchant-payment facts is one sale, not two. Rejected/prepared/reserved attempts and retries emit no completed-flow signal. Replay uses recorded observations; never reclassify history with a new classifier.

Manifest v1 installs these four channels, with explicit source categories:

- Merchant spending: settled player-register purchases and both directions of NPC goods commerce, excluding fees/secondary-item imputed value. Link refunds to original sales and subtract their recorded goods principal. Never classify transfers, deposits, withdrawals, interbank movements, interest or fee recognition as goods sales.
- Credit: actual fixed-loan principal disbursed and revolving principal drawn, less actual principal repaid. Undrawn facilities/limit increases, interest capitalization, debt sales and creditor write-offs are not new principal flow. Repayment allocation supplies principal explicitly. Existing funded-bank rules remain authoritative.
- FX demand: settled source-principal value sold and target-principal value bought, excluding fees and excluding internal treasury/denomination/bookkeeping conversions. Include an NPC customer's explicit FX conversion once. Record both sides at the captured conversion publication; failed quotes create no demand.
- Monetary flow: explicit operator issuance/retirement and NPC goods-payout issuance/player-payment retirement, separately identified. Cash withdrawal/deposit/serial replacement merely changes representation and is neutral. Count NPC creation/retirement only once across account/cash/clearing representations. Institutional capital bootstrap is historical infrastructure funding, not a recurring activity source or a fifth channel.

Observation IDs derive from world + original operation + classifier version + source ordinal. Indexed uniqueness rejects duplicates. Aggregate by observed financial day/currency/source and keep immutable raw facts for bounded history. Store amounts as checked signed integers; sums exceeding a bound reject before the source transaction commits, never wrap or silently saturate actual money. Normalized *signals* may saturate as defined below.

Use trusted accounting time at admission, monotonically bounded by the worker's retained UTC observation. An ordinary effect at boundary B belongs to [B,B+day). A delayed servicing commit whose original obligation predates an already sealed observation bucket is observed in its current accounting day, retaining original effective time as provenance; do not reopen closed windows. Recovery reproduces the original attribution. This late-observation rule changes neither debt due dates nor financial postings.

Maintain indexed open-day counters and capacity summaries; never scan accounts/journal history every update. At most 11 current currencies, 23 institution summaries and 16 source classes per currency/day. Derive lending-capacity proxy from non-distressed banks' free CASH above operating floors, excluding segregated reserves/claims/customer deposits. Attribute each actual cash account once in its held currency. It is a normalization proxy, not a new eligibility or funding promise. Snapshot changes with affected monetary commits and sample a bounded close value per day. Index capacity changes by captured accounting time/frontier: historical closes must not read a current balance containing observations assigned to a later day.

## Deterministic model v1

Technical defaults below implement the selected equal weights, signs, windows and caps. They are configurable only through immutable future revisions; they are not additional gameplay approvals or claims of real-world macroeconomic calibration.

All normalization uses activation-anchor CAD value: major units × anchor rate(currency) / anchor rate(CAD), as exact rationals. Live FX changes cannot create apparent activity by revaluing old observations. CAD is a measurement unit, not currency backing. The activation manifest must contain CAD and every enabled currency. A newly added currency requires a future manifest/anchor extension, not guessed history.

Let W be the last up to seven fully observed completed days, n its covered count. B is up to seven fully observed completed days immediately before W. No missing days are invented. Equal temporal weights apply within each covered window; a flow contributes while retained, at most seven rate publications. Empty W with complete zero-activity coverage returns the prior rate vector/prime unchanged regardless of capacity or B. Missing required coverage is unavailable, not zero.

Define positive configurable daily normalization floor Q = 1,000 anchor-CAD, a technical tuning default, and `clamp(x) = max(-1,min(1,x))`. Exact v1 signals per currency:

| Signal | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Spending S | With per-covered-day net goods means a in W and b in B: `clamp((a-b)/(abs(a)+abs(b)+Q))`. With no baseline coverage, S=0. Refunds retain signed attribution. |
| Credit C | `(drawPrincipal-repaidPrincipal)/(drawPrincipal+repaidPrincipal+0.01*meanFreeCapacity+n*Q)` over W. Nonnegative gross components; signed result lies within [-1,1]. |
| Money M | `(created-retired)/(created+retired+n*Q)` over W. Operator and NPC subtotals remain visible. |
| Exchange X | `(boughtValue-soldValue)/(boughtValue+soldValue+n*Q)` over W. Both sides valued from original captured principal converted into anchor-CAD. |

Prime pressure `P=(S+C+M)/3`; new APR is `clampTo[0,0.25](oldAPR+0.001*P)`. APR step 0.001 is 0.10 percentage points. Round final APR HALF_EVEN to six fractional APR digits (four fractional percentage-point digits), retaining exact pressure in provenance. Check final bounds and step after rounding. Ordinary loans capture loan-currency policy; old fixed/subprime contracts are unchanged.

FX pressure `h[c]=(X[c]-M[c])/2`. For a single rational scale alpha in [0,1], candidate rates are `old[c]*(1+alpha*0.005*h[c])/(1+alpha*0.005*h[reference])`. Reference remains exactly 1. This compares all currency pressures, including reference-currency activity, without independent inconsistent pair edits.

Choose the largest feasible common alpha respecting **every ordered currency pair**: new/old pair ratio in [0.995,1.005], new/activation-anchor pair ratio in [0.5,2], and representable positive reference rates in [10^-12,10^6]. Constraints before rounding are linear rational inequalities in alpha after positive denominators are cleared. Publish rate scale 12 HALF_EVEN. Recheck every ordered pair after rounding; if needed use a deterministic 64-step rational bisection from zero to the candidate scale, retaining the largest witnessed valid rounded vector. Prior valid vector is the zero-scale fallback. Never clamp currencies separately or claim reference-rate step checks establish pairwise bounds. Reject corrupt/out-of-range prior state instead of pretending it is a valid fallback.

Prime/FX configuration weights are nonnegative rationals summing to one per model; zero denominator/invalid config fails validation. Policy revisions bind Q, capacity factor, weights and numeric contracts. Model v1 meanings remain installed for history/backlog. No random macro inputs, synthetic investors or autonomous rate recovery.

## Observation and publication ordering

Fixed work retains existing priority.

A partial pre-A day is not a complete zero day. Startup installs no fake historical activity. Inherit configured prime/latest FX at activation; if prime is outside 0%–25%, reject A8 activation with an operator explanation rather than clamp history. Existing future legacy prime edits conflict explicitly with takeover; do not discard them. Paused/manual mode still closes windows and records unchanged or explicitly scheduled outcomes, preserving chronology; resume uses current W, never a burst of deferred automatic rate moves.

## Trader identity, catalogs and ticking

Use A7 `BranchPlans.id(world,Source)` for generated villages and **slot 0** for required-local-bank currency. Match the assigned workstation against the captured original village bounds expanded 32 blocks; overlapping matches choose nearest source anchor, then lexical stable ID. For bankless/player-built settlements, select the nearest persisted market anchor within 64 blocks of the workstation in the same dimension, tie by ID; otherwise create an anchor at that workstation. Market UUID derives from world/dimension/anchor coordinates and a version tag. Never merge/recenter saved markets by a changing POI cluster. No workstation/market yet: defer assignment until an actual trade needs initialization, then capture the trader's current block as its fallback anchor and server currency. Later workstation assignment follows the approved relocation rule.

Village currency is captured from slot 0, or server currency if no bank exists at initialization; later banks, denomination changes and foreign branches do not change it. Moving traders retain stock/restock progress and adopt a new market only on authoritative workstation assignment. Do not infer membership from a nearby kiosk, banker profession or client-supplied market ID.

Wandering markets derive from world/entity UUID. Choose currency from a captured sorted enabled manifest using SHA-256 of version tag, stored world seed, world/entity UUID and rejection-sampled bounded index; persist result. No reroll on travel/reload. Market lifetime is the trader lifetime, with immutable trade history retained after despawn.

Read vanilla generated catalogs on the server. Detach exact item/data-component predicates, result identity, ingredient quantities, catalog XP and original `maxUses`. Emerald output becomes a money payout; emerald cost becomes money debit; retain non-emerald secondary ingredients. This replacement must not create a new offer for emerald merchandise or introduce extra trade catalogs. Unsupported dynamic/component predicates are unavailable with an explanation, never silently widened.

Stock cap uses original offer `maxUses`; stock belongs to individual trader/offer. Player-sale acceptance ignores vanilla purchase-use quota. Village restock counter accrues only loaded ticks with WORK activity, valid assigned profession workstation and villager within 2 blocks of its workstation; preserve progress across restart/relocation. On 10,000 eligible ticks, refill sale offers and subtract one period; no offline/sleep/game-time catch-up. Wandering offers never restock. Suppress competing vanilla stock/demand restocking under A8 ownership.

Market recovery accrues once per market/server tick if at least one assigned living trader is loaded, never once per trader. For an item deviation d at the last real trade/explicit price-source epoch, T=24,000 eligible ticks, elapsed t: `d(t)=d*2^-floor(t/T)*(1-(t mod T)/(2*T))`. This deterministic piecewise-linear interpolation halves exactly per T without a floating exponential. Ordinary checkpoints/restarts preserve that original deviation/time anchor and accumulated eligible ticks; they never re-anchor the formula or change its half-life. A trade computes current recovery first, changes demand, then captures a new deviation/time anchor. Bound market multiplier to [0.5,2]. Persist eligible progress at most every 200 buffered ticks and before a trade/restock outcome; conservative loss of an unacknowledged buffer is not offline progress. Bound queued checkpoints; pressure suspends the affected service with a reason rather than inventing elapsed ticks.

## Price arithmetic and quote identity

Temporary source is configurable 1 CAD per emerald using original emerald quantities, converted through captured effective FX into market currency. Standard quantity for an item is the smallest positive goods quantity among its canonical vanilla catalog entries; freeze that quantity/source manifest once for the model revision. Offers with distinct result components/secondary predicates have distinct keys; never erase enchantments or other components to share a price. No A9 recipe scan or imputed ingredient price.

Each standard quantity changes demand by exactly 0.01 base-multiplier units, positive for a player purchase and negative for a player sale. Use progressive marginal pricing through a batch. Equivalent same-instant split and combined orders, without intervening state changes, have equal total exact principal and final demand. Preserve a per-market/item/player/side fractional-minor carry so CEILING charges/FLOOR payouts apply cumulatively rather than penalizing or benefiting click splitting; capture/reset carry only through explicit price-source/policy epochs with recorded residual handling.

If a carry produces zero incremental payment for a proposed small order, reject/re-quote a sufficiently large supported batch before item consumption. Never fulfill a free purchase or seize goods for a zero payout to preserve split equivalence. Carries do not cross actors, sides or component-distinct goods.

Apply deterministic reputation/discount sources already produced by the villager, without vanilla demand (A8 owns it). Collapse sources into signed r in [-0.25,0.25]. Establish a neutral ask a and bid b with `b<=0.8*a`, retaining larger original gaps. For favorable r, jointly limit the benefit to `t=min(r,(0.8*a-b)/(0.8*a+b))`; for unfavorable r, t=r. Adjust ask to `a*(1-t)` and bid to `b*(1+t)`. This preserves the 20% gap and ensures good reputation never worsens either neutral quote, nor bad reputation improves one. At an already-minimum gap, no simultaneous favorable adjustment is possible; the selected 'up to 25%' is limited by available spread. Never lower a favorable player's bid below its neutral value merely to fund an ask discount. Recheck equivalence across reachable same-market offers/reputation witnesses, limiting the common favorable factor further where needed. For one-sided catalogs use a hypothetical comparison principal from that entry's normalized base solely for the cap; do not add an offer. Match component/ingredient equivalence explicitly, conservatively rejecting an unresolvable comparison. After conservative rounding enforce final payout <=0.8*ask and positive representability; if one legal trade cannot pay a positive minor unit, offer a larger supported batch or explicit unavailable amount, never a free purchase or zero-value item seizure.

Quote expires within 30 seconds and no later than any FX/pricing witness. Server binds session/entity/distance (<=6 blocks), loaded/alive/current offer, player/item/account authority, all revisions, requested quantity and output capacity. Revalidate before financial preparation. Client actions select only server-offered options; no client-supplied prices, reputation, currency, stock, account authority or bank issuer.

Use narrow server-owned NPC ISSUE/REDEEM authority with named source/retirement facts, never a fabricated player merchant or funded-bank account. A bounded private NPC settlement account per currency may exist only as a compositional bridge, zero at terminal settlement, excluded from customer account caps/wealth and unavailable as lending capital. Reuse A4 typed clearing; neither debit bank capital nor treat NPC unlimited funds as bank funding. Input goods, secondary ingredients and cash use existing custody reservation, independently witnessed Minecraft save, then original financial commit. Commit money, stock/demand, observations and delivery intent atomically in SQLite; physical delivery is separate. Cash uses `CashIssuance` serial conservation/original lot IDs; at most 24 lots and existing 64-record budgets. Require output/change capacity before admission, preserve exact reservations and recheck before delivery. No ground-drop fallback or implicit account switch. Unrepresentable cash change rejects with a usable explicit alternative.

Delivery/recovery uses original custody IDs, marked output and independent persisted delivery/save evidence. Resolve original operation before retry; committed money never repeats and a replayed receipt never materializes a second item. Ambiguous physical delivery retains claim/quarantine and bounded operator explanation, not automatic duplicate compensation. No exactly-once cross-store claim. Entity death/unload invalidates new trade admission but does not erase an existing receipt or delivery obligation.
