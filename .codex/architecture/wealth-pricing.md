# Wealth, pricing and funded deposit yield — A6-04

## Authority and bounded valuation

`WealthValuation` reads at most four open owned BANK balances, counting the posted amount once regardless of holds/reservations. TRADING has no installed monetary/securities product; physical cash, delivery claims, items and real estate are excluded. The built-in yield source reads eleven deterministic depositor RECEIVABLE identities; bank-owned receivables and depositor income are not additional player assets.

Installed `Source` adapters provide an authoritative owner summary of ASSET or LIABILITY components with stable economic claim IDs, source revision, mark ID/time and explicit availability. All installed sources are required. Principal, posted/accrued interest, payable fees and uncollectable unpaid debt remain liabilities until the owning debt contract explicitly extinguishes them. A creditor write-off cannot remove borrower debt. Duplicate economic claim IDs fail rather than double count. Each request is bounded to eight sources, 64 total components and 10,000 input UTF-8 bytes; future portfolios must supply revisioned summaries, not an unbounded scan.

One latest A2 publication supplies every component conversion. FX must be no later than valuation and at most 48 real hours old; required securities marks must not be future or older than 1,200 seconds. Exact rational comparison applies the literal 100,000,000 current-denomination threshold without a rounded display promoting eligibility. A denomination revision invalidates old quotes. Asset/liability components, account identity/currency/revision, latest immutable posting sequence, provider revision, mark/source identifiers, FX rates/publication and policy are captured in the canonical valuation witness. Posting sequences prevent a debit/credit round trip from reviving an old quote.

`ValuationSources` registers one required source contract per startup turn, with stable world/manifest/source operation identity. Persisted source contracts are compared bidirectionally with immutable registration facts. Additions are journaled; deleting the projection, removing a required adapter, or changing its kind/freshness contract fails closed before catch-up/READY. A present adapter returning unavailable data instead yields an unavailable snapshot, retains the last displayed net worth/Tier and denies new Tier2 benefits. Protected read/repay/withdraw/close services remain under ordinary protections. Structural removal of an installed historical adapter requires restoring its contract, including an explicit unavailable stub if necessary; it cannot silently uninstall liabilities.

Production extensions use `FoundationRuntime.Configuration.production(clock, calendarConfig, sources)`. This composes built-in yield assets plus supplied sources and shares the same valuation across admission, daily reevaluation and yield rates. The default `ServerServices` constructor uses its runtime's configured valuation; an explicitly injected `ServicePricing` must use the same contracts. `WealthValuation`, `Source` and `SourceSnapshot` expose valuation integration without pretending a securities market is installed.

## Tiers, quotes and configuration

`TierReevaluation` evaluates established owners in ordered pages through stage 7, once per world financial day. Complete below-threshold snapshots immediately deny sensitive admission and publish a factual downgrade; unavailable snapshots preserve the last displayed designation with an unavailable flag. Fresh synchronous snapshots recheck every new Tier2 admission/quote. Hybrid future deposit rates change at the next boundary; specialist below-threshold accounts earn zero future APR and accept only protected existing-fund/debt actions. Same-currency new credits/transfers and FX target credits cannot bypass specialist deposit restrictions; refunds, repayments and withdrawals remain distinct protected actions. Production provider lifecycle also requires the completed institution-capital manifest.

`ServicePricing` quotes bind owner/world, supplying institution, service, principal/fee, Tier, policy, quote revision, underlying valuation and expiry. Lifetime is the earlier of 60 seconds and the underlying A2 expiry. Commit requires the same current witness; relevant balance, posting, provider, mark, liability, denomination, FX or policy changes require a new quote. Protected withdrawal/closure FX may fall back to an explicitly displayed Tier1 fee when valuation data is unavailable; missing/stale FX still cannot convert money. No unavailable valuation grants Tier2 or a factual downgrade.

| Policy | Tier1 | Tier2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FX source-principal fee | 0.50%, CEILING once to source minor unit | zero |
| Future executed trading cash notional | 0.25% | zero |
| Deposit nominal APR | 0.50% | 1.00% |

Only hybrid/specialist institutions offer Tier2 products. Retail-only providers retain Tier1 pricing even for a qualified owner. `CumulativeTradingFee.Accumulator` retains per-order exact earned fee and posts only incremental cumulative rounded fee across partial fills; cancellation cannot refund earned executions. No actual execution or security positions exist here.

`PricingPolicy` defaults prime to 6.00% APR. `Change` accepts 0..50 percentage points inclusive, at most four fractional percentage-point digits, rejects invalid input, and schedules only a strictly future world-day boundary beyond wall/high-water/frontier. Up to 16 pending policies are retained; old effective rows are immutable. A1 admission owns authorization and original-operation replay. Prime/rate APIs are for future origination; fixed credit contracts are not rewritten and no lending is installed.

## FX and cash settlement

`FxQuotes.Quote` retains its old constructor and adds an optional pricing witness. Assigned account FX is revalidated through the shared price book; a legacy free quote becomes stale if its source is assigned, closed, rebased or otherwise revised. Explicit unassigned legacy accounts retain their earlier transitional path. Source principal is fully converted under A2 rounding; fee is an additional source-currency debit. `InstitutionSettlement` resolves typed institution identities and existing customer BANK targets, enforces ordinary holds and normal balances, and rejects overflow/unknown roles. `FeeSettlement` credits the bank's CASH and INCOME with depositor EXPENSE recognition, rather than using the global fee pool as bank earnings.

Cash preview contexts are server-owned. Assigned FX cannot use an old context-free reader. Deposits explicitly split each foreign cash subtotal into principal plus rounded source fee; full principal converts, and the fee's cash redemption shares the same validated consumed physical quantity. Withdrawals debit converted principal plus fee while physical issuance backs only the cash principal. Unexecuted/returned groups pay no fee. Capture and revalidate saved quote IDs/witnesses at RESERVE/COMMIT. Same-currency service has no FX fee; specialist deposits still require current qualification.

A priced deposit accepts at most four exchange currencies per batch to preserve record/payload and menu budgets. Gross cash and full-close budgets must exactly equal principal plus the rounded fee. Some minor-unit budgets have no solution; reject with an explicit fresh-amount request rather than overcharge, waive or silently deduct from a fully quoted principal. `FxRequests.quoteForClosure` / `LedgerService.quoteClosure` solve the exact protected closure budget. Existing A3/A4 closure claims/holds/primary/currency checks and bank proof remain mandatory. Preview text displays principal, fee and credit/debit.

## Time-weighted yield and money

`DepositYield` activates once at a captured UTC instant after shared calendar setup. Three production providers are installed: stage 1 `economy:deposit-yield`, stage 1 `economy:deposit-yield-payout`, then stage 7 `economy:a6/tier-reevaluation`. They use one child per worker turn and pages≤16. Empty domains have no daily events. Immutable claim `created_ms` starts a new account prospectively, even years after calendar activation; persisted APPLYING events survive their final target until completion. World origin supplies day/month alignment. Downtime creates ordinary bounded catch-up.

Every authoritative monetary/lifecycle commit is decorated after original A1 lookup and trusted handler preparation, before record validation/insertion. `FoundationStore.decorateCommit` and the raw `SqliteStorage` observer let runtime, fixed-system and domain writes share the hook; wrappers must delegate it. Historical effective time comes from trusted economic/deadline facts; manual commits use the worker clock. Account posting changes, provider switches, closure and rebase update time integrals atomically with the original finance/outcome. No physical side effect is replayed by yield.

Claim key is account+bank+currency. It retains principal, weighted major-unit milliseconds, exact18-place accrued amount, prior-close compound basis, recognized minor receivable/payable, paid total, captured APR/period start, creation time, due total, distress and revision. Integrate `(posted principal + signed prior-day unpaid carry) × elapsed milliseconds`; divide by 1,200,000, then APR/364. Current-period earned interest does not compound before day close. The combined eligible basis cannot be negative; signed HALF_EVEN carry offsets later earnings. Arithmetic uses 34 significant digits/HALF_EVEN and 18 fractional major digits. New funds earn only their funded interval; holds are counted once. Boundary rates read historical Tier after the preceding stage 7, without applying an old Tier for an extra day.

Accrual uses matched bank EXPENSE+/PAYABLE− and depositor RECEIVABLE+/INCOME− recognition. The synthetic `economy:depositor/<owner UUID>` system party consumes no BANK slot and is not an institution cash source. Payout uses bank PAYABLE+/CASH− and customer ACCOUNT+/depositor RECEIVABLE−. Bank expense and depositor income remain recognized after payout. Only the bank's own free CASH can fund it; segregated RESERVATION balances, receivables, income and customer deposits cannot. Interest may use the operating expense floor but cannot spend protected escrow. Treasury funding conversions use captured A2/A4 clearing without player fees; conversion facts retain publication, currencies/units, exact reference residual and typed rounding identity. Overflow or unavailable/insufficient funding defers rather than inventing cash.

At each 28-day boundary, persist a dated due tranche for newly payable minor units. Retry only that dated amount, oldest boundary then stable account order, retaining later nondue accrual. A month56 promise cannot be folded into month28's timestamp. Deferred outcomes complete clock work while retaining payable/distress; any unpaid dated promise blocks future new credit/increases through `DepositYield.distressed`. There is no automatic replenishment or depositor haircut.

Provider/currency changes preserve old claims separately; old earned amounts cannot disappear or be reinvested early through switching. Ordinary explicit settlement pays dated promises, including captured-FX payment into an owned different-currency account. Actual closure realizes its prorated active segment and retained claims into the protected destination, with explicit signed residual disposition. Base closure postings and additional payout use one overlay/commit, avoiding overwritten destination balances. More than 16 historical whole claims or the 64-record budget may require prior ordinary monthly settlement. Residual-only closure uses16-row facts up to the fixed 23×11=253 keys/account; overflow/corrupt excess fails closed rather than truncating. A rebase preserves old-currency claims and identities; later payout converts explicitly. No extra account slot is assumed.
