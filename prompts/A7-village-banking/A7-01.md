4. Define deterministic selection streams and captured plan revisions, denomination capture point, stable village/branch IDs, optional omission reasons and required-provider readiness. Generation cannot wait for financial storage readiness; specify durable handoff/reconciliation.
5. Define loaded-only observations and command inspection/pause/retry scopes.
6. Specify staffing tick persistence/pause semantics and future NPC admission without implementing staff. Reserve desk geometry/anchors in templates so A7-04 does not depend on A7-05 classes.
