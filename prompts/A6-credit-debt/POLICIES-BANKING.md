# Approved A6 banking policies — D10, D11, D14, D16, D17

Status: **approved by the user on 2026-11-04** under the [consolidated policy approval](POLICY-REVIEW.md). This is the normative D10/D11/D14/D16/D17 contract. Money is current server-currency major units unless explicitly native. Pricing, deposit yield, underwriting and capital use the approved [credit policy](POLICIES-CREDIT.md), including the 28-billion seed. No implementation or verification is claimed.

## Wealth and Tier 2 — D10

- Qualifying net worth is the sum of all owned authoritative ledger assets, including available/held/reserved balances counted once, plus published supported securities valuations when their adapter is installed, minus all outstanding borrower liabilities (principal, posted/accrued payable interest and assessed payable fees). Do not subtract held/reserved funds twice. Loan/card proceeds and corresponding liabilities enter the same snapshot and cannot create net worth. A pending transfer does not count in both accounts.
- Initial A6 supported assets are ledger money only. Physical cash in inventories, item-delivery claims, arbitrary items, real estate, speculative holdings and uninstalled integration assets are excluded until converted/deposited into supported ledger assets. This avoids trusting copied cash lot markers as proof of unique owner wealth. Owned holdings are not silently treated as zero when an installed required valuation adapter is unavailable: retain the last displayed snapshot, flag it unavailable, and deny new Tier 2 admission until a fresh complete valuation succeeds.
- Debt collectability and net worth are separate. Uncollectable debt remains a liability while the authoritative borrower liability is outstanding; remove it only through explicit approved borrower-debt forgiveness/extinguishment, not merely a creditor receivable write-off. A creditor may recognize its accounting loss while the unpaid historical borrower debt still reduces net worth. Collection expiry is not implicit wealth creation. Debt payments reduce both assets and liabilities once.
- Use one immutable latest A2 FX publication per snapshot. A publication is fresh if its UTC effective instant is no more than 48 real hours old and no later than the valuation instant; this uses UTC because A2 publishes daily UTC, not each financial day. Installed securities marks must be no older than one financial day (1,200 seconds); future marks reject. A valuation quote expires at the earlier of 60 real seconds and its underlying A2 quote expiry (separate from the 30-second servicing/payoff quote), and binds asset/liability/account revisions, FX/mark IDs, denomination revision and policy version. Revalidate all at settlement.
- Evaluate each established player once per financial day through bounded scheduled pages, and synchronously check a fresh snapshot at Tier 2-sensitive admission. At exactly 100,000,000 or above qualify; below it immediately downgrade. There is no grace period, grandfathering or balance-only shortcut. If a valuation source is unavailable, deny new Tier 2 benefits rather than declaring a factual downgrade from stale data. Read, repay, withdraw and close remain available under ordinary protections.
- At hybrid banks, downgrade changes future fee quotes/deposit accrual to Tier 1 at the effective next daily boundary; new quotes cannot claim zero fees once fresh admission is below threshold. Existing fixed-rate loans/cards retain their signed rate and debt; new products use current eligibility. A quote is invalidated by changed eligibility before commit.
- At specialist Tier 2 banks, below-threshold players retain accounts and liabilities, with read/repay/withdraw/close service only. No new account, borrowing, card issue/replacement, deposit or trading benefit until eligibility returns. Existing usable cards are restricted to permitted read/repayment actions. A preapproved replacement needed solely for repayment is unnecessary because repayment remains available through the kiosk/menu without the physical token.
- A denomination change keeps the literal 100,000,000 threshold in the new server currency, recalculates all net worth using the committed rebase FX snapshot and invalidates outstanding eligibility quotes. Do not silently preserve a prior Tier 2 designation.

## Institution accounts, primary onboarding and migration — D11

The approved cap remains four open BANK accounts total per owner, including primary, with one open BANK account per currency. A secondary account opens only in the chosen institution's home currency at its kiosk. A bank's ownership/capital is a system institution party, not the player placing its block.

### Initial primary and legacy mapping

- If the world has no established denomination, its first-player chooser selects it through the existing approved protocol. New players use that established world denomination; they cannot individually change it. Their permanent primary is first opened at a Tier 1/hybrid kiosk whose home currency equals the world denomination. Login, a generic card, an ATM or a teller cannot create the player-facing account. Until that visit, account-dependent features show “Visit a <currency> bank branch to open your primary account”; the chooser itself creates no additional primary. The primary is permanent/protected after creation. Every supported currency has at least one Tier 1 issuer.
- Existing accounts retain ID, owner, currency, funds, claims, history and primary status. Migration adds an explicit UNASSIGNED_PROVIDER state; it does not choose a bank from an old terminal's color or fabricate an earlier bank relationship. Existing read/payment/deposit/withdrawal operations retain their previously authorized behavior during migration; new opening/closing/card/credit actions require assignment. This transitional exception is limited to accounts that predate A6 and is journaled/versioned, not a new-account bypass.
- The owner assigns each legacy account by visiting a Tier 1/hybrid kiosk with the same home currency, accepting the displayed provider and committing an idempotent assignment. A Tier 2 specialist is also selectable only if current wealth qualifies. Assignment consumes no account slot, transfers no money, preserves primary protection and records the selected issuer. Two simultaneous assignments race on the same expected revision; one wins.
- A previously rebased primary remains protected. If its current currency has a Tier 1 issuer, assignment requires such an issuer; supported A6 currencies all do. No automated reassignment occurs on migration, login or failed assignment.
- After a later authorized denomination change, the existing primary keeps its institution and identity while its currency follows the server denomination. This is an explicit **primary-denomination product exception** to an institution's normal native-currency secondary products. A bank's ATM/teller still dispenses/accepts its home-currency cash; FX is quoted when serving that non-native primary. This exception preserves world-currency primary behavior without silently changing its bank. UI labels issuer home currency and account currency separately.
- Existing old card collectibles remain generic access-only. They do not acquire an institution or credit facility during account assignment. A new issued debit card is offered free after assignment using the normal issuance/custody protocol.

### Provider switching

Switching is not closing a protected primary or creating a fifth account. It preserves account ID, currency, balance and primary flag, and changes provider through a dedicated versioned operation:

1. At the source bank kiosk, owner requests a switch-out authorization bound to account, target issuer, expected revision and operation ID. It expires after seven financial days. No financial hold or fee is created; cancelling/expiry has no balance effect.
2. At the named target bank kiosk before expiry, owner confirms. Target home currency must equal account currency, and current target/Tier eligibility must pass. A non-native rebased primary can therefore switch to an institution matching its current currency. Both kiosks must be visited; one cannot impersonate the other merely because currencies match.
3. Atomically revalidate source authorization/account/target eligibility, change provider, consume authorization and revoke the old debit token. Offer one new debit card through normal issuance. The provider change is free. No change to debt creditor, loan contract, credit-card issuer or facility; existing facilities stay attached to their issuer and may be paid from the switched account.

An outstanding switch authorizes only its exact account/target. Source account closure, a conflicting switch, new denomination revision or altered account revision invalidates it. Trusted recovery/maintenance can resolve a damaged assignment only through an audited operator migration operation with recorded reason; it cannot waive balances/claims/primary protection.

## Issued card lifecycle and advances — D14

- Account opening, first debit/credit token issuance, annual maintenance and card closure fees are zero. Owner-requested replacement costs 25 server-currency units, converted to the selected funding account currency through a captured FX quote and checked ceiling debit. Charge only when the new issuance commits; insufficient funds rejects without revoking the currently usable token. Apply any required FX pricing from the shared fee table, with a single combined preview. Do not charge to the credit facility itself.
- One active debit token per BANK account and one active credit token per facility; all copies referencing a token share its one authoritative status, facility limits and transaction checks. Physical clone uniqueness is not claimed; copying an item cannot duplicate credit, and revocation invalidates every copy. Up to four debit tokens follow the four-account cap. A player may hold at most three open credit-card facilities across all issuers; aggregate exposure limits still apply. Replacement retains facility and account IDs, score, statement anchor and outstanding debt.
- Issued tokens expire five financial years (1,820 days) after issuance. Expiry revokes token use, not the underlying account or debt. Renewal is a replacement at the issuer kiosk, free within 28 days before expiry or after natural expiry; replacement for loss/damage before that window costs 25. The old token is revoked in the same issuance transaction, and the new expiry begins at successful renewal/replace issuance. Token delivery uncertainty follows A5 rather than charging twice.
- Owners may report a token lost remotely through their authenticated account menu; revocation is immediate/idempotent and free. A replacement still requires the issuing bank's kiosk. Cards are not transferable borrowing authority. No virtual credit-card spending in this release; normal authorized account reads, transfers and repayments remain usable without a token. Public token labels reveal issuer/network/product/expiry, not private account UUIDs or owner secrets.
- Cash advances are requested at an ATM or teller using an active owned credit token and a confirmed advance quote. Advance funds first settle from lender to the owner's selected open BANK account, with a matched credit receivable, then physical cash withdrawal uses the separate A5 custody protocol if selected. An ambiguous cash delivery cannot cause a second credit draw. No advance fee, annual fee, over-limit fee or late fee is added in this initial release; approved interest and FX pricing still apply.
- Advance principal may not exceed the smaller of 20% of that facility's assigned total credit limit and its currently available overall credit, measured in facility currency. The limit is a maximum **outstanding advance principal**, not a fresh 20% allowance per request. Existing advance principal consumes it until repaid; accrued interest consumes overall available credit but does not raise the advance sublimit. No overdraft/over-limit draw. Principal limit reductions cannot erase debt or force an unapproved fee.
- Facility contractual currency is fixed at origination. The first unestablished card is denominated in the then-current server currency and has its exact 2,500 limit even if the issuer's home currency differs; credit products are permitted this explicit reporting-currency denomination. Later credit-card facilities and bank loans are denominated in their issuer's native currency; apply the shared server-currency principal/limit ceilings through the captured downward conversion at origination. Subprime contracts use the then-current server currency. Disburse into a selected owned BANK account in contract currency, or explicitly quote the funding FX leg into another owned account; borrower debt stays in contract currency. No silent conversion or assumption of an extra account slot. Later server denomination changes do not redenominate existing debt/limits; show raw facility currency plus current display conversion. They cannot reset starter entitlement, age or statement cycles. Any future contract redenomination needs a separate approved protocol.

## CHF opening publication and physical change — D16

- Use the deterministic **game seed 1 CHF = 1.600000000000 CAD**. This is an intentionally chosen game calibration, not a current/historical market quote. In an existing world, introduce CHF at the next UTC A2 publication boundary with reference-units-per-CHF = that same publication's reference-units-per-CAD × 1.6, rounded half-even to A2 scale 12. Capture that publication/baseline in the new version and never resample historical days. Fresh worlds include the same CHF/CAD baseline in their approved opening publication.
- Apply A2's existing default variation/pull/band parameters to the CHF opening baseline. Existing currencies and their historical rows are untouched; unavailable prior publication gates CHF activation. Prior publications explicitly lack CHF rather than synthesizing backwards history. Registry/catalog activation is atomic with the new effective FX version/publication readiness.
- All approved CHF note/coin denominations are enabled in the catalog; physical terminal owner configuration can restrict a subset. ATM uses notes only; teller uses notes and coins. Existing lot/serial/custody requirements apply. Electronic CHF retains cents precision; there is no automatic five-cent electronic rounding.
- Cash withdrawal must be exactly representable with the enabled denominations. Reject 1.02 CHF as unrepresentable rather than dispensing 1.00 and silently changing the debit. A teller may offer an explicit alternative preview (for example 1.00 CHF withdrawal while 0.02 remains in the account); only confirming that new amount starts a withdrawal. No automatic change-credit or invented coin. Deposit counts the exact accepted notes/coins and credits exact nominal value before any explicitly quoted FX fee. Original note artwork and operator-only unmarked-mint trust remain unchanged.

## Branded terminals, kiosk geometry and migration — D17

- Every bank has ATM/teller/kiosk variants labelled with issuer and home currency. Branded ATM and teller cash denomination allowlists contain only that institution's home currency; ATM accepts/disburses bills, teller accepts/disburses bills and coins. Both may service any of the player's owned BANK accounts, including another issuer's account or a non-native primary, by an explicit captured FX preview where cash/account currencies differ. No extra interbank surcharge or cash withdrawal fee; ordinary FX fee/tier pricing applies (Tier 1 FX 0.50%, Tier 2 FX zero under the approved credit policy). Same-currency service has no FX fee. Account opening/closing/replacement still requires the correct bank kiosk; cross-bank cash service grants no lifecycle authority.
- Preserve the existing ATM/teller 2×2-cell geometry, anchor, support, rotation, generation and single-drop behavior. The new branch kiosk is one cell wide × two cells high × one cell deep, with lower anchor/block entity and upper satellite, horizontal facing, full sturdy support below the lower cell, and the same loaded-only validation/no-forced-chunk/single-drop principles. Break either cell or lose support: invalidate the generation and drop one same-brand kiosk item in survival, none in creative. Unloaded cells defer integrity checks rather than being deleted. Reach remains six blocks.
- Existing ATM/teller base recipes remain as neutral unbound hardware recipes. Disable generic dye recoloring as a path to currency/issuer changes; old dye-variant IDs continue to resolve. New branch-kiosk neutral recipe is a crafting-table 3×3 grid: `iron_ingot glass iron_ingot` / `iron_ingot redstone iron_ingot` / `iron_ingot chest iron_ingot`, yielding one unbound kiosk. No recipe produces money, a funded account or an issued usable card.
- Player placement creates new disabled unbound terminal settings. The placer/owner or operator selects one of the 23 approved bank brands through the existing authenticated terminal settings surface, then explicitly enables valid native-currency settings. This design permits players to construct branches but confers no institution capital/underwriting authority. Owning a kiosk does not waive its customers' account/credit/Tier checks. All branding resources use the chosen original bank palette, not the old item's color.
- Bank binding is immutable for that placement generation once enabled. Rebranding requires disabling, proving all terminal-local pending intents/custody effects resolved, removing normally and placing a fresh generation; no dye or client component mutation. Outstanding owner claims remain attached to their original cases, not destroyed with the machine. Drops can retain decorative bank identity for appearance, but placement always creates fresh owner/generation/settings and requires explicit server confirmation; item NBT never imports financial authority.
- Legacy placed ATMs/tellers retain block/item IDs, geometry, anchor, owner/settings, inventory and outstanding reservations/claims. Migration adds UNBOUND_BANK, retaining prior settings as legacy metadata. Old committed/pending operations reconcile using their stored pre-A6 semantics; no new cash/FX/lifecycle operation starts on the unbound terminal. Once its pending operations are safely terminal, owner/operator chooses a bank and confirms a displayed native-currency allowlist change. This is a journaled, idempotent migration fact. Unassigned colors do not map automatically to banks, and no machine is deleted or converted to a new currency silently.
- Generic unplaced legacy dye variants remain valid items and become neutral/unbound hardware upon new placement. They are not counterfeit valid branded branches. All 23 native-currency bindings, new card counts, CHF denominations and registry size must replace old hard-coded ten-currency/118-denomination validation bounds explicitly, with bounded payloads and measured fixture evidence rather than unbounded expansion.

## Acceptance examples and tradeoffs

1. A player with 100,000,000 supported ledger assets and no liabilities qualifies; a 5,000,000 loan raises assets and liabilities by the same amount and leaves net worth unchanged. A player holding copied cash markers cannot claim Tier 2 from inventory items. The tradeoff is that cash-rich players must deposit before qualifying.
2. Four accounts across four distinct currencies are valid. A fifth, or a second CHF account at Newhaven while an open Lakeward CHF account exists, rejects. Visiting Newhaven cannot close the Lakeward account. Selecting Lakeward for an existing unassigned CHF account keeps its ID/funds and consumes no new slot.
3. A rebased Crownstone primary now denominated in CHF remains a Crownstone primary. Its Crownstone ATM still dispenses CAD; a CHF-account debit for CAD cash requires an explicit CHF→CAD quote. No forced provider change or world reset occurs.
4. Tier 2 falls below threshold after a real loss: new benefits stop immediately on authoritative admission; specialist service becomes read/repay/withdraw/close-only. Signed fixed-rate debt is not repriced. This trades a grace period for deterministic avoidance of short-lived threshold parking.
5. A 2,500 facility has at most 500 outstanding advance principal; after advancing 300, only 200 additional advance principal is available even if overall credit remains. A replayed original advance returns the same draw, not another 300.
6. A failed 25-unit replacement debit leaves the original token usable; an uncertain successful replacement is looked up under its original operation before any new charge. Renewal never resets account age, debt or starter entitlement.
7. With CAD as permanent reference and CAD rate 1, opening CHF is 1.600000000000; with CAD rate 0.75 reference-units/CAD, opening CHF is 1.200000000000. No historical CHF rows are fabricated and no current market rate claim is made.
8. A loaded old purple ATM with pending delivery retains its exact case and cannot be repurposed to CHF during reconciliation. After resolution its owner explicitly chooses Lakeward, confirms CHF-only cash settings and gets the original branding appearance. The tradeoff is a one-time owner migration visit instead of an unsafe guessed bank mapping.
