# Approved debt servicing, scheduling and Collection Agent policies

Status: **approved by the user on 2026-11-04** under the [consolidated policy approval](POLICY-REVIEW.md). This is the normative D01–D05, servicing D14–D15 and D13 contract. Approved APRs, calendar, starter credit, terms, kiosk rules and reporting durations remain binding. No code, assets or tests have been produced.

## Time, calculation and same-instant order

- Financial day = 1,200 elapsed UTC seconds, including downtime. Persist the world financial origin at the first A6 calendar activation transaction; never reset it on restart. World score boundaries are origin + k×33,600 seconds, k≥1. A facility statement/installment anchor is its committed origination instant; card statement ends and loan installment dates are anchor + k×33,600 seconds. First card minimum is due 16,800 seconds after first statement; first loan installment is due one month after origination. Report at each due instant +2,400 seconds; default at oldest unmet due instant +100,800 seconds (84 financial days). Boundaries are UTC instants, not calendar dates or local midnight.
- For each effective instant process, in order: financial policy/FX boundary publication; completed prior-period interest and deposit yield; statement creation; opted-in scheduled payments and loan/card minimum satisfaction; overdue/default/collection expiry transitions; due+2 reporting; score calculation; tier reevaluation; entity eligibility publication. Periodic jobs use the version effective for that boundary. Physical agent work is a subsequent live-server effect.
- A preview or worker acceptance timestamp does not reserve historical deadline eligibility. Manual payments revalidate in the serialized financial transaction against its authoritative effective UTC instant and current state. Process all due scheduled boundaries at or before that instant before admitting a new manual mutation; scheduled autopay within a boundary runs before that boundary's reports/transitions. Therefore a new manual payment at the exact default/expiry instant cannot preempt that transition. It may instead obtain a fresh post-default collection quote if still collectible. An earlier received but uncommitted request cannot backdate after a committed transition. A manual payment committed after due but before due+2 may still cure the report under the approved window; it never changes historical audit time. At collection expiry, expire first: no payment committed at or after expiry revives or extends collection. Never use client timestamps or physical UI response time for these decisions.
- Persist effective policy versions, boundaries, exact input snapshots, operation identities and residuals. Use decimal arithmetic with 34 significant digits, HALF_EVEN for interest/rate intermediates; store accrued amounts at 18 fractional major-unit digits with HALF_EVEN. Ledger interest/fees/yield settle HALF_EVEN to currency minor units; carry the signed residual with its originating account/bucket, never into a different account. Card minimum and nonfinal scheduled installments use CEILING to a minor unit, capped by actual debt. Final payoff quotes include rounding-adjusted residual settlement; record any waived subminor residual as a bounded explicit rounding fact, not a phantom payable amount. Reject overflow, never clamp balances.
- Each card bucket accrues daily on principal plus unpaid bucket interest at APR/364; a day is each complete 1,200-second period from its facility anchor. Statement boundaries are after that day's interest. Payment at a day boundary follows completed-day accrual. Between full daily boundaries, no card daily charge is earned yet; paying in full before the next cutoff stops that next charge. Never round the periodic percentage before multiplication. Monthly loan accrual uses APR/13 on the segmented balance described below; only interest from previous completed periods may enter the compounding base, not current-period unposted accrual. Monthly compounding and repayment are distinct from daily card compounding.

## Card minimums, arrears and repayment

For statement j, let P be all unpaid purchase/advance principal, I be interest earned in that statement period, D be the full posted debt at statement close, and A be total unmet minimum requirements from older statements after credited payments. Let newMinimum = min(D, ceilMinor(0.02×P + I)). The newly stored requirement is min(newMinimum, max(0, D-A)); the displayed total required is A plus that new requirement, capped by D. Preserve A≤D as a service invariant; any debt correction/forgiveness/full payoff atomically reduces or discharges affected requirements so their sum never exceeds remaining repayable debt. Store the old arrears and new obligation separately; do not create a second monetary liability for a minimum requirement. Zero debt yields zero minimum; there is no extra fixed minimum or late fee. Previously billed unpaid interest remains debt and is not rebilled as new I.

A payment's amount satisfies oldest unmet contractual minimums first, capped by their still-unmet requirement; the same amount cannot satisfy both an old and new requirement. Financial allocation pays the debt attributable to the oldest overdue statement first (its interest, then its highest-APR principal), then other unpaid interest, then highest-APR principal. Within equal APR: cash advance before purchase, then oldest origination/statement ID. A requirement is cured when its required amount has been satisfied; paying a positive amount below it reduces arrears but does not cure it. Overpayment is rejected at confirmation with a fresh exact-payoff quote; never silently debit more or convert excess to spendable credit. There are no late/over-limit/annual card fees in the initial product.

Do not create new obligations by adding an already carried minimum to its own outstanding amount. Each original requirement retains its due instant and unpaid amount. The age of the oldest unmet requirement drives delinquency/default. Full current payoff satisfies all outstanding requirements and stops further accrual. New-card spending requires an OPEN facility and available committed limit, independent of the item texture.

## Loans, payoff and payment execution

For each installment, use the approved amortization schedule with APR/13 and 13×years periods. At a due boundary settle the accumulated current-period interest exactly once, capitalize only unpaid prior-period interest, then apply scheduled payment. An insufficient payment is partial, not a successful installment: satisfy overdue interest/principal for the oldest installment first, then later interest/principal. Prior unmet installments carry their original dates. Monthly interest on previously capitalized overdue interest uses the same contractual APR; no penalty APR or late fees. Default is driven by the oldest unmet amount, not a count reset by token payments.

Early principal repayments have no fee. After old interest/arrears are satisfied, excess reduces principal immediately; keep the contractual installment amount and shorten remaining term, adjusting final installment, rather than silently extending/restarting the loan. Maintain one authoritative current-period accrual accumulator for every loan, not just payoff quotes. Before every principal-changing payment, rate-effective boundary or month close, add simple pro-rata interest for the elapsed segment: (principal + previously capitalized unpaid interest) × r × elapsedSeconds/33,600. Exclude current-period unposted interest from that base. Partial payments must settle/retain earned accrued interest before reducing principal; then begin a new segment at the new base. At month close settle the accumulator once and reset only its completed portion; only unpaid completed-period interest enters the next period compounding base. A constant balance for a full period therefore earns exactly base×r, while early principal repayment preserves interest earned on the larger old balance without charging it twice. The final actual installment/payoff is capped at remaining principal+interest; no negative residual or new fee. A fresh full-payoff quote freezes its amount at its calculation instant for at most 30 real seconds, invalidated sooner by any relevant state/policy revision or scheduled accrual/default/expiry boundary. If successfully committed within that window, explicitly waive only the additional unposted intra-period interest earned between quote creation and settlement, record the exact waived amount as an audit fact, and close the debt. This small customer-favorable waiver applies only to successful full payoff, not partial payments, mere previews or expired quotes. A quote cannot straddle a daily card charge, monthly loan close, default or collection-expiry boundary. This makes the displayed amount payable without silently increasing the authorized debit; a stale quote requires new confirmation.

Manual repayment is always available through authorized remote banking/branches and Collection Agent when collectible. Manual payments may fund from any selected owned BANK account: if currencies differ, show an explicit captured A2 FX conversion and current Tier fee, debit principal plus fee and settle the exact contract-currency payment atomically. Only the amount reaching the facility satisfies its debt/minimum; the FX fee does not. Validate funding FX/quote revisions together with the payoff state so an account cap or a rebased primary cannot strand repayable debt. No automatic new account or implicit overdraft is created. Autopay is **opt-in per facility**, defaults OFF and uses one named owned BANK account in the debt currency; no automatic FX, overdraft, account substitution or debt refinancing. Card options: required total minimum (default selection when enabling autopay), immutable statement balance less subsequent payments, or current full payoff. Loan autopay covers the total due installment arrears, capped by debt. At each due boundary, all-or-nothing debit the selected amount if funds suffice; otherwise record failure without creating a debit. Retry once per financial day for two days after the original due date, stopping when its selected autopay target is satisfied, on opt-out, closure or default. Persist the target mode and originating statement/installment identity for that due window. For minimum mode, the target is the remaining required minimum; for statement mode, it is that statement's unpaid balance after all later payments; for full-payoff mode, it is the freshly computed full remaining debt at each retry, with explicit prior consent to that mode and sufficient funds. Satisfying the contractual minimum alone does not stop retries for a still-unpaid statement/full-payoff target. Full-payoff retries stop when debt is zero; new spending after a target is completed belongs to a later due window. Manual payments reduce the retried amount. Stable installment/date/attempt IDs prevent duplicate debit; payment processing precedes due+2 reports at the same instant. Balance after cutoff does not cause endless retries. Disconnect/being offline does not disable opted-in autopay.

## Default, reporting, sale and expiration

- At default stop all new draws and close credit access; freeze the collectible debt at its current principal+posted interest+earned interest through that instant. No post-default interest, new late fees or collector fees. Origination records and repayment access remain. Exposure and net-worth adapters still see unpaid debt.
- Record at most one report per original due obligation plus append-only corrections. At due+2 report paid/current if cured by that instant, otherwise missed with original due amount and remaining unmet amount. Delinquency does not reset original due dates; a later cure updates status via correction without deleting the original audit event. Credit history can distinguish late-then-cured from still unpaid.
- Default reports expire exactly defaultInstant +7×436,800 seconds, independently of collection. Paid defaults remain as **paid default** until that date; their unpaid amount becomes zero and future score recovery can proceed under the scoring policy. No new negative default event each month for the same account. Credit-report visibility excludes expired entries while audit/ledger records persist.
- Sale transfers creditor ownership once under the bank funding/sale policy. A cash-sale funding failure leaves the frozen receivable in a collections-pending state owned by the original creditor, with the same original default/expiry dates. A collection case and agent may service that creditor; do not delay the legal/gameplay clocks or fabricate sale proceeds. Sale later does not reset either expiry. Never duplicate the player's obligation.
- Collection expiry = most recent qualifying acknowledgement instant +2×436,800 seconds, initially anchored at default. A committed positive partial payment strictly before expiry, while debt remains, resets that expiry; full payment settles the case instead. Clicking/viewing/exit/refused/zero/failed payment never acknowledges it. At expiry make the remaining debt uncollectable: no new demands, agent, interest, required payments or accepted collection payments. It cannot revive through later attempted payment. Record creditor loss/write-off separately from preserved historical borrower liability; no automatic score erasure. A future forgiveness product needs separate approval.
- Payments cannot settle at or after expiry through a stale quote. A payment committed before expiry remains valid despite a delayed UI response. Report expiry, death cooldown, collectability and financial repayment statuses are independent persisted dimensions.

## Collection Agent gameplay

One nonhostile agent per online debtor aggregates eligible cases. It follows only that debtor, never attacks, opens doors, breaks/places blocks or pushes entities. It has 20 health, normal player-targeted damage susceptibility, no drops/XP and no despawn loot effect. No spawn eggs or recipes. It cannot be leashed, ridden, bred, traded for goods or moved by portals as a debt transfer. In peaceful mode it remains a nonhostile NPC.

Try spawn when the debtor has collectible debt, no live agent and no active kill cooldown. Search at most 16 already-loaded candidate positions in an 8–12 block horizontal ring, within ±3 blocks vertically; require safe solid support, two free blocks, no liquid/fire/cactus/portal and unobstructed placement. Reject if the player is dead, spectator or changing dimension. A failed search retries after 200 server ticks; never generate/force-load a chunk. A per-player timer plus a global budget of four search candidates per tick bounds work. Select candidates deterministically from case/player/world identity plus attempt number; restarting cannot erase a committed cooldown.

Walk speed 0.25 blocks/tick, path refresh at most every 20 ticks, follow until 3 blocks away. Beyond 32 blocks or after 200 ticks of failed pathfinding, attempt the same bounded safe relocation at most once per 200 ticks. No collision pushing, attacks or block interaction. Right-click requires the debtor, a live current case/menu/session and distance ≤6 blocks. An 8-second initial greeting plus one reminder at most every financial day while within 8 blocks is displayed only to the debtor; no global chat spam or per-tick noise.

Any observed combat death, including environment damage after spawn, commits a per-debtor cooldown of seven financial days before replacement. Unloading/logging out/server stop is not a kill: remove/reconcile the presentation without a new cooldown. Dimension changes remove the old presentation and use the safe-spawn process in the player's new loaded dimension; no duplicate instance. If death persistence is uncertain, conservatively retain a provisional seven-day no-spawn window until authoritative case reconciliation. Entity identity is never the debt record.

Base A6 does not integrate an external claim/protection mod. The agent obeys vanilla restrictions and never modifies blocks; no external protected-region API is assumed. Expose a default-allow server integration predicate for spawn/interact suppression. If that predicate denies an area, suppress/relocate presentation without forgiving debt or resetting cooldown/clocks. This is an API boundary, not a bundled integration.

## Acceptance witnesses

- At score/report instant T, a scheduled successful payment is processed before its due+2 report; one report and one score-cycle outcome exist after restart.
- A statement whose D=100 and older requirements A=99 stores at most1 of new requirement even if the raw new minimum is2; full payoff clears every requirement. A statement with P=1,000, I=10 and older unmet minimums=15 has newMinimum=30 and total required=45, capped by actual debt. Paying 20 satisfies the old15 plus5 of the new requirement; it cannot satisfy the same 20 twice.
- A loan with principal1,000 and r=1% earns5 interest over the first half-month; a500 principal reduction at that point preserves that earned5, and the second half earns2.5 on the remaining500, total7.5 for that month when prior accrued interest is paid. No intra-month interest-on-interest is added.
- A partial cure leaves the original due instant; at +84 days the remaining unmet obligation defaults once. Defaulted debt stops accruing. Full payment changes report status to paid default until the original seven-year expiry.
- At collection expiry−1 second a committed positive partial payment resets the two-year window; at expiry or later it rejects and cannot revive the case. Neither action resets the seven-year reporting date.
- Killing, restarting and reconnecting cannot produce an agent before the persisted seven-day cooldown. Unloading cannot create a second case or duplicate agent.
