# A3-05 — Rebase mapping and integration handoff

Execution draft. Requires explicit invocation and the [shared execution contract](CONTRACT.md).

## Prerequisites

Verified A3-04 and A2-05 confirmed intent and denomination revision contract.

## Deliverable

Plan from trusted confirmed intent and current revisions. Keep each permanent primary ID and map it to the target currency. Merge a colliding BANK account into the primary and retain its retired ID with an audit successor link. Preserve unrelated foreign BANK accounts and exclude trading and other products. Block the entire rebase if an affected account has an active hold, restricted funds or an unresolved obligation. Hand the mapping, required transactional revalidation and atomic posting, account and denomination effects to A4.

## Acceptance gate

Verify collision and no-collision cases, preserved foreign accounts, stale revision denial, affected versus unaffected claims, rejection when ledger preconditions are unavailable, and repeatable planning. Measure bounds with a world-scale fixture and document limits for A4. Record verification and the next-package handoff.

## Exclusions

Live rebase, conversion calculation, posting, denomination mutation and historical rewrites.
