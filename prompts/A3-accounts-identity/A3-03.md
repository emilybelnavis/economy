# A3-03 — Lifecycle and limits

Execution draft. Requires explicit invocation and the [shared execution contract](CONTRACT.md).

## Prerequisites

Verified A3-02.

## Deliverable

Define trusted opening and closure eligibility contracts. Serialize ownership, expected revisions, currency uniqueness, cap and protection checks. Protect the permanent primary and any BANK account in the current world denomination. Retain closed IDs for audit. Block closure on active holds, restrictions or unresolved obligations. Require a distinct open same-owner BANK destination for funded closure. Hand settlement and transactional revalidation to A4 and teller-only access to A5. Keep live player opening and closure gated until A5.

## Acceptance gate

Verify concurrent duplicate and last-slot opens, protection, stale or foreign destination rejection, blocked obligations using fixtures, same-ID retry, interrupted write rollback and audit retention. Record verification and the next-package handoff.

## Exclusions

Physical teller access, funded settlement, operator mutations and rebase.
