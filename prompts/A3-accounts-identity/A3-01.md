# A3-01 — Parties and authorization

Execution draft. Requires explicit invocation and the [shared execution contract](CONTRACT.md).

## Prerequisites

Verified A1.

## Deliverable

Define world-scoped typed owner references, retain Minecraft player UUIDs, and distinguish authenticated actors from account owners. Reserve organization identifiers for future integrations. Derive player, system and operator authority from trusted server context. Define an audited operator-only inspection boundary for A3-04. Check authorization before returning original operation outcomes.

## Acceptance gate

Verify self-access, cross-owner and cross-world denial, trusted internal authority, spoofed actor and operator rejection, and durable audit identity across restart. Record verification and the next-package handoff.

## Exclusions

Organization registration, grants, account storage, money, operator mutations.
