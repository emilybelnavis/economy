# A3-02 — Account catalog and product constraints

Execution draft. Requires explicit invocation and the [shared execution contract](CONTRACT.md).

## Prerequisites

Verified A3-01, A2 currency registry and A2 denomination read contract.

## Deliverable

Define stable account IDs, owners, purposes, revisions, currency eligibility and retained closed identities. Create the permanent primary on first authorized banking use after denomination selection, using the current world currency at the serialized decision point. Count it within three distinct-currency BANK accounts. Define one separate trading funding identity per player outside that cap. Hand trading currency eligibility to B2 and hand A4 the rule rejecting incoming account-based payments before primary initialization without creating an account or moving funds.

## Acceptance gate

Verify before-selection rejection, idempotent concurrent initialization, restart stability, uniqueness constraints and bounded owner lookups over closed history. Record verification and the next-package handoff.

## Exclusions

Teller access, player-facing payment, trading currency policy, balances and live rebase.
