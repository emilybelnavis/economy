# A2-03-02 — FX diagnostics

Approved. Prerequisites: verified A2-03-01 and [shared contract](CONTRACT.md).
Owner: A2 FX operations. Extend A1 diagnostics with a bounded authorized
read of awaiting selection, reference, last committed day and policy, next
due day or backlog, and waiting, retryable or unavailable reason. Separate
a pending settings revision from the last effective revision. Read consistent
committed snapshots on the existing worker and return detached values.
Diagnostics must neither advance the clock nor start repair or publication.
Use the A1 authorization and overload behavior. Exclude new admin settings,
unbounded history, and any separate scheduler or store.

Gate: verify preselection, healthy, overdue, backward-clock, invalid
configuration, overload and blocked publication views. Exercise access
denial and prove inspection leaves publication state unchanged. Hand off
diagnostic evidence to A2-03-03.
