# A2-05 — World denomination and change protocol

Approved. Prerequisites: verified A2-04 and [shared contract](CONTRACT.md).
Owner: A2 chooser and denomination protocol. Wire the first connected
player's selection to the atomic A2-03 opening contract. Proposed: track
eligible connected players in server-observed join order and bind the
chooser session to world and actor. Validate the choice and lease before
admission. Disconnect before acceptance transfers the chooser immediately.
After acceptance hold the chooser until the original write resolves. Commit
keeps the choice. Definitive failure allows reassignment. Recover unknown
outcomes with the same operation identity. Publish committed state only.

Later changes require two explicit confirmations by the same authorized
admin for the same world, target and denomination revision. The five-minute
window expires sooner on disconnect, permission loss, restart or revision
advance. Proposed: start that window at the first confirmation and validate
expiry, permission and revision again at authoritative processing. Hand
off the change intent to A4, which must commit the denomination revision
and account effects atomically. A3 specifies foreign-account preservation
and target collision merges. The permanent reference stays fixed.

Display original historical amount and currency first. Keep any frozen
converted display separately labeled with currency and rate date. Proposed:
include the source rate-set identity in frozen provenance. Never silently
relabel recorded amounts.

Gate: verify real chooser entry and handoff, midnight opening and immediate
FX availability after commit and due-day catch-up, competing/retried
selections, disconnect on either side of acceptance, restart and uncertain
commit recovery. Exercise wrong actor, expired or revoked confirmation,
concurrent admins and stale revisions with a fixture for the A4 boundary.
Verify original history and frozen display survive a denomination change.
Exclude balance transformations, live rebases with financial effects and
physical cash.
