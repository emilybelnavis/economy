# A2-02 — Conversion and settlement rounding

Approved. Prerequisites: verified A2-01 and [shared contract](CONTRACT.md).
Owner: A2 conversion domain. Deliver pure, deterministic conversion functions.
Accept validated, same-publication rates expressed in the permanent reference
per major unit. Multiply the source minor amount by source rate and target
scale, then divide by source scale and target rate. Keep exact intermediates.

Approved: group deposits by source code and FLOOR once per subtotal. Credit
payable groups and report zero-credit groups left with the player. Reject an
all-zero deposit. CEILING the required withdrawal account debit for the
requested cash amount. Display a nonzero historical magnitude below one
minor unit as less than one minor unit. Proposed: preserve its sign and
reject the full request on invalid input or overflow. Distinguish missing
rates, malformed inputs, out-of-range results and zero total credit. Deposit
and withdrawal inputs must be positive. No mutation, permission decision,
network or storage in the conversion functions.

Gate: USD 20 to CAD 27.92 and JPY 1,000 to CAD 8.87 using the CAD fixture.
At 75 JPY per CAD, two CAD cents grouped together credit JPY 1. At 150 JPY
per CAD, withdrawing JPY 2 costs two CAD cents. Check inverse and cross
orientation, mixed payable/skipped groups, all-zero, tiny signed display,
exact zero, overflow and invalid rates. A4 owns posting and A5 owns cash
retention and delivery.
