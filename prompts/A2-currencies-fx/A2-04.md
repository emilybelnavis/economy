# A2-04 — Versioned quotes and pair history

Approved. Prerequisites: verified A2-03-03 and [shared contract](CONTRACT.md).
Owner: A2 quote and history services. Use server-authenticated, bounded
requests and seeded published rates for acceptance. Bind quotes to actor,
world, directed pair, side, requested amount, published rate and pricing
versions, expiry, fee currency and final debit and credit. Initial spread
and fees are zero. Quote lifetime is 60 seconds at most, ending when the
applicable rate or pricing policy changes or the server restarts. Proposed:
server-held bounded quotes with exclusive expiry. Handle absent rates and
uninitialized FX explicitly.

A4 must check freshness at the start of authoritative trade processing.
An expired queued trade moves no funds. A valid trade can finish committing
after expiry. A successful trade consumes the quote atomically with posting.
Rollback leaves the quote usable while valid. Retry of a committed operation
returns its original result before checking quote freshness or consumption.
Different operation IDs cannot spend the same quote twice. A2 defines this
handoff and verifies quote behavior without moving funds.

History uses one committed rate set per day for direct, inverse and cross
pairs. Return newest first and pin paging to the first page's upper date.
Proposed: bounded cursor scoped to world and directed pair, with an exclusive
continuation date, max 100 rows and explicit end marker. Return explicit
missing-day results and diagnose unexpected missing committed data.
Verify rollover, queued expiry, restart, actor mismatch, original-operation
retry and single-use contract through a fixture. Verify stable paging across
publication, malformed cursors, missing dates and bounded reads. Exclude
financial postings and actual quote consumption in production.
